About this version

Picks are stored only in this browser. The schedule and available odds use ESPN public feeds. Lines are checked when the app opens or returns to the foreground; coverage is not guaranteed. Preliminary projections are experimental. Completed games are recorded when ESPN reports final scores, and only picks locked before tipoff can be graded. Export CSV to keep a backup.
